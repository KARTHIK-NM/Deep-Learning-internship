Architecture Summary

  • Backbone: MobileNetV2 (ImageNet Transfer Learning, Inverted Residual Blocks)
  • Input Resolution: 224 × 224 × 3 RGB tensor
  • Augmentation Layers: Random Flip, Rotation (±15°), Zoom (±10%), Contrast Adjustment
  • Classifier Head: GlobalAveragePooling2D → BatchNorm → Dense(128, ReLU, L2) → Dropout(0.3) → Dense(12, Softmax)
  • Fine-Tuning Strategy: Initial freeze for head warm-up, followed by fine-tuning top 30 depthwise-separable convolutional layers with Adam optimizer (lr=1e-4)
